# Screenshots

Images used by the root `README.md`. They show the fictional **Fernhill Bakery** example (`examples/fernhill-bakery/`) opened through the page's own
"Open project file" control, in a light-mode browser at 1200 px wide, with IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono. The step screenshots are cropped to the top of each
step; the page continues below them. The two `sheet-page-*` images are pages 1 and 2 of the exported PDF, rasterized at 80 dpi.

They were made on 2026-10-14 from the page built at that date and are not regenerated by any test, so they can drift from the page. Retake them after a visual change.
