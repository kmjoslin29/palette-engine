# Screenshots

Images used by the root `README.md`. They show the fictional **Fernhill Bakery** example (`examples/fernhill-bakery/`) restored from browser storage (so the header reads
"Restored from this browser"), in a light-mode browser at 1200 px wide, with IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono. The step screenshots are cropped to the top of each
step; the page continues below them. The two `sheet-page-*` images are pages 1 and 2 of the exported PDF, rasterized at 80 dpi.

All step images were retaken on 2026-11-20 from the page at pe-update67, with IBM Plex Sans and Plex Mono loaded into the page (the earlier set had been rendered with a fallback font despite the note above). `step-3-existing-vs-proposal.png` is the Tertiary 1 card cropped to its top, after Suggest a fix for color-blind views on Fern Green and Make it the proposal. `step-2-analyze-palette.png` adds Oat #E8DCC4, Crust Gold #F2C14E and Plum #793974 to the sample. The two `review-page-*` images are pages 2 and 3 of the palette review PDF for that same state, rasterized at 80 dpi; the `sheet-page-*` images are unchanged, because the sample's brand color sheet is byte-identical.

The images are not regenerated by any test, so they can drift from the page. Retake them after a visual change.
