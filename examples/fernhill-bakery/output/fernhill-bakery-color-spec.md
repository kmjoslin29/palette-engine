## Method and limits

- Screen: sRGB is canonical. A color beyond sRGB is flagged "P3 only" and mapped in by reducing OKLCH chroma; the original is kept.
- Print: values come from lookup tables built with LittleCMS from the GRACoL 2013 coated (CRPC6), SWOP 2013 (CRPC5), GRACoL 2013 uncoated (CRPC3), PSO Coated v3 (FOGRA51), PSO Uncoated v3 (FOGRA52), Coated FOGRA39L (VIGC 260) profiles (relative colorimetric + black point compensation). They agree with LittleCMS to ΔE00 0.15 (95th percentile) and 0.99 (worst case) across all six. Swatches are approximations, not proofs. Out of print gamut when the round-trip ΔE00 is above 2.0 (convention).
- Spot: Pasted by the designer; Palette Engine bundles no Pantone data. 6 entries scored by ΔE2000 in Lab D50, Coated and Uncoated separately. Names carry "(approx.)" and none has been checked against a physical guide.
- Accessibility: WCAG 2.2 AA is the pass/fail gate. APCA is advisory, web only, never applied to print. Color-blind views use Machado et al. 2009, full severity; roles collapse below ΔE00 10.
- Evidence tiers: well-established (standards and measurement science), supported but contested (peer-reviewed effects that depend on context), low confidence (weak evidence or an engineering choice), speculative (convention).
- Pantone is a trademark of its owner. Palette Engine bundles no Pantone data.
